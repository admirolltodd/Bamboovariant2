Useful answers, minus the corporate fog

Questions?

The basics about menus, locations and online ordering. For item-specific or allergy-sensitive questions, contact the restaurant directly.

Do all three Bamboo locations use the same menu?

The locations share the same general menu structure, but availability can differ. Use the live ordering menu for the location you plan to visit.

Why are there no prices on this website?

Prices are kept on the ordering platform so guests see current location-specific information rather than an outdated static copy.

How do I make sure I order from the correct restaurant?

Use the Order page or Locations page. Each button goes directly to Crestview, Fort Walton Beach or Niceville.

Can I place a larger group order?

Start on the Catering page, then contact the location nearest your event so they can confirm timing and availability.

Can the website guarantee ingredient or allergy information?

No. Menu descriptions are helpful references, not a substitute for speaking with the restaurant about allergies, cross-contact or recipe changes.

Where should I check current availability?

The live ordering menu for your chosen location is the best source for what can be ordered right now.
