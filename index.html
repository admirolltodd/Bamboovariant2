03

Started in a 45-seat room.

Bamboo was founded in October 2007 by Joe Rice and Danny Ledford, after years working in restaurants in Destin. The idea was simple and slightly stubborn: real sushi and real hibachi, at a price people could actually pay on a Tuesday. They started with forty-five seats in Crestview.

Word got around. Crestview expanded, Fort Walton Beach opened, Niceville followed in 2015. Three locations later, it’s still the same family behind the bar and the line — fast-paced, a little loud, and genuinely fun.

Come in as a customer and leave as a friend.
