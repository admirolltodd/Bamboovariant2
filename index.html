03 / Local Since 2007

Built here, not dropped here.

Bamboo started in Crestview in 2007 with a small dining room and a simple idea: make sushi and hibachi approachable enough for a regular weeknight, not just a special occasion.

That local formula grew to Fort Walton Beach and Niceville. The look of this site is intentionally cleaner and louder than a traditional Japanese steakhouse aesthetic, but the point is the same: make the food the center of attention.

Three towns.
One name.
